DEFINE 4.0 HACKATHON PROJECT

EmergencyPass: Smart Emergency Private Vehicle Assistance System

Over 150,000 trauma fatalities occur annually in India due to delays in the critical "Golden Hour". Over 40% of emergency patients are rushed to hospitals not in ambulances, but in private cars or taxis — stuck in gridlock without priority, with no hospital pre-warning, and without legal green corridors.

EmergencyPass bridges this gap with an intelligent end-to-end multi-stakeholder ecosystem:

🚨 1-Tap SOS Activation

Captures high-precision GPS, selects closest trauma facility based on live ICU capacity, and initiates routing.

🏥 Hospital Pre-Arrival Notification

ER triage rooms receive incoming vehicle telemetry and live countdown, preparing resuscitation bays in advance.

🚗 Road Vicinity Alert Radar

Surrounding motorists within an 800m corridor receive audible yield warnings to safely clear lanes.

👮 Police & Anti-Abuse Audit

DigiLocker vehicle authentication, AI route deviation tracking, and immutable audit logs prevent misuse.
