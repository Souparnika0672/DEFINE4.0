Privacy & Location Data Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Location Tracking Consent & Activation

EmergencyPass captures GPS coordinates strictly upon explicit user activation of an Emergency SOS transit. Location tracking terminates immediately when the trip is marked completed or cancelled by the user or hospital personnel.

2. Data Minimization & Retention

We do not retain continuous high-frequency location points indefinitely. Telemetry updates are automatically expunged after 24 hours. Only trip-level audit records (timestamps, start/end status, destination hospital) are preserved for safety and audit compliance.

3. Role-Based Access Control

Patient and vehicle live telemetry is disclosed only to verified receiving hospital triage desks, authorized police tactical responders, and the operating driver.
