Terms of Service & Legal Disclaimer

Academic & Coordination Prototype Notice

IMPORTANT REAL-WORLD LIMITATIONS: EmergencyPass is an emergency coordination prototype designed to reduce transit delays. It is not an official government emergency dispatch service. For life-threatening emergencies requiring direct ambulance dispatch, call 108 / 112.
1. Driver Responsibilities & Traffic Laws

Activation of an emergency trip does NOT grant immunity from traffic laws, speed regulations, or traffic light compliance. Drivers must always prioritize safety and comply with official road authority directives.

2. Routing & Free Public Mapping Services

Routing geometry and estimated travel times are powered by public OpenStreetMap and OSRM services. Real-world traffic fluctuations and unforeseen road closures may affect actual travel times.

3. Anti-Misuse Policy

Intentional misuse, false emergency activations, or repetitive unauthorized broadcasts are subject to administrative review and account restriction.
